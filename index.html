About OTPforge

OTPforge v2.0 — a modern, private two-factor authenticator.

  • TOTP & HOTP · SHA-1 / SHA-256 / SHA-512 · 6–8 digits · Steam Guard
  • QR scan, otpauth:// import & manual entry
  • Everything is stored locally in your browser — no account, no cloud.
  • Compatible with Google Authenticator, Authy, Aegis & any RFC-6238 service.

Tip: use Menu → Export backup regularly to keep your accounts safe.
