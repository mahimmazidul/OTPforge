About OTPforge

OTPforge — a free, private two-factor authenticator.

  • TOTP & HOTP · SHA-1 / SHA-256 / SHA-512 · 6–8 digits · Steam Guard
  • QR scan, otpauth:// import & manual entry
  • Everything is stored locally in your browser — no account, no cloud.
  • Compatible with any RFC 6238 service and standard authenticator apps.

Questions & answers

What is OTPforge?

OTPforge is a free two-factor authentication (2FA) authenticator web app. It generates standard time-based one-time passwords (TOTP, RFC 6238), entirely in your browser.

Does OTPforge work offline?

Yes. Codes are computed locally on your device with Web Crypto, so OTPforge keeps working without an internet connection.

Where are my secret keys stored?

Only in your browser's local storage. They are never uploaded to any server, and you can export a JSON backup at any time.

Can I import accounts from another authenticator app?

Yes. Use your current authenticator's transfer/export feature, then scan or paste the migration QR into OTPforge and every account is imported.

Tip: use Menu → Export backup regularly to keep your accounts safe.
